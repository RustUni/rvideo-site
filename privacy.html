Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

RVideo ("the app", "we") is a desktop video editor for macOS. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes. In short: RVideo runs on your Mac, we do not operate any server for it, and we never receive your videos, your account details or your login tokens.

1. Information stored on your Mac

  • Your projects: the videos you import, subtitles, voice-over audio and exports are saved in folders on your Mac.
  • App settings: preferences, the developer app IDs you enter (for example a TikTok client key) and the history of videos you published are saved in a local database on your Mac.
  • Secrets and tokens: API keys, client secrets and the access and refresh tokens of connected accounts are saved in the macOS Keychain.

None of this is sent to us.

2. TikTok

If you choose to connect a TikTok account, RVideo opens TikTok's own login and consent page in your browser. With your permission the app uses:

  • Basic profile (user.info.basic): your TikTok open ID, display name and avatar URL, only to show which account is connected. They are stored in the local database on your Mac.
  • Video upload (video.upload): when you click Publish and choose TikTok, the app uploads the video file you picked from your Mac directly to TikTok, where it appears in your TikTok inbox as a draft. You finish and post it in the TikTok app. The app stores the upload status locally.

RVideo does not read your followers, messages, likes, other videos or any other TikTok data.

3. Other services you may use

Some features talk to online services only when you use them. Your Mac connects to these services directly, and their own privacy policies apply:

  • YouTube (Google) and Facebook (Meta): when you connect an account and publish a video to it.
  • Google Gemini: if you add your own Gemini API key, subtitle text and video titles are sent to Google to translate them or write captions.
  • Google Translate: used for translation when no Gemini key is set.
  • Video links you paste: the app downloads the video from the site you linked.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or share your information.
  • We do not show ads and do not include analytics or tracking.
  • We do not post anything to your accounts unless you click Publish.

5. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your Mac until you delete it. Clicking Disconnect for an account in RVideo deletes its tokens from the Keychain and removes the account from the app. You can also revoke RVideo's access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services permissions. Uninstalling the app and deleting its data folder (~/Library/Application Support/com.localvn.rvideo) removes everything else.

6. Children

RVideo is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly handle their information.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
