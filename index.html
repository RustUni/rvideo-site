Edit videos on your Mac and share them where you post.

RVideo is a desktop app for macOS. It adds subtitles and voice-over to your videos, cuts short vertical clips, and exports them ready for social platforms.

What it does

  • Transcribes speech on your Mac and lets you edit and translate the subtitles.
  • Adds a voice-over generated on your Mac.
  • Exports full videos and short 9:16 clips.
  • Optionally sends a finished video to accounts you connect: your TikTok inbox as a draft, your YouTube channel, or a Facebook Page you manage.

How your data is handled

RVideo runs on your computer. There is no RVideo server and no RVideo account. Connected accounts are stored in your Mac's Keychain. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
